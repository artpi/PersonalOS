01 / THE CONNECTIVE TISSUE

Different apps.
Common ground.

A highlight you saved. A task you need to finish. The box with your camera gear. They belong to your life. Their data should live together, too.

PersonalOS stores them as native WordPress Knowledge records. Each app adds its own view and behavior; compatible plugins and tools can work with the same underlying content.

04 / A DIGITAL HOME, ON YOUR TERMS

Apps come and go.
Your knowledge stays.

These plugins add ways to work with your data. The records live in WordPress, and remain there when a PersonalOS plugin is deactivated or uninstalled.

User-created records are private by default, with WordPress controlling access. Sync services and AI providers are connections you configure. Stuff photos use standard WordPress Media URLs; private records do not make those files private.
